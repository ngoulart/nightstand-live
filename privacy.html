NightStand privacy policy

A sleep tracking app for iPhone.

What the app records

  • Phone movement. The app reads the iPhone's motion sensor and keeps 30-second summaries of how much the phone moved.
  • Sleep from Health. If an Apple Watch writes sleep to the Health app, the app reads those sleep records to show your nights. Nothing else is read from Health, and nothing is written to it.
  • Morning check-ins. Your rating of the night, your guesses of when you fell asleep and how long you slept, your answers to four short questions about the night, and the night the app then showed you.
  • Tests. The results of the 30-second tests you take, in the morning check-in or any time.
  • Charging and battery. When the phone is plugged in or unplugged, and the battery level.
  • Screen lock state. When the phone is locked or unlocked while the app is running.

Location. If you allow it, the app uses the iPhone's location service only to keep running in the background, so the night's recording has no gaps. It does not store your location, and you can turn this off in the app's Settings at any time.

The app does not record which apps you use or what is on your screen. It has no account and asks for no name or email.

Where it stays

On your phone. The app sends nothing to us or to anyone else, so we do not receive, sell or use any of it, for advertising or otherwise.

Exporting your data

Settings has an Export button. It writes a file with your nights, hourly movement and unlock counts, check-ins and test scores. The file goes only where you send it.

Background activity

With location allowed, the app keeps running in the background. Otherwise iOS wakes it briefly now and then so it can keep recording movement. Neither shows anything on screen.

Your choices

  • Permissions. Movement, sleep from Health, location and notifications each have a switch in the app's Settings. The iOS permissions can also be withdrawn at any time in the iPhone's Settings. The app then reads nothing from those sources.
  • Deleting your data. Delete the app. Everything it kept is deleted with it.
