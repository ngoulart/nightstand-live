NightStand privacy policy

The iPhone app of our study on sleep and phone use at night.

Who uses the app

People who joined our study and consented to it in the study's survey. The app is not for anyone else.

What the app collects

  • Phone movement. The app reads the iPhone's motion sensor and sends 30-second summaries of how much the phone moved.
  • Sleep from Health. If an Apple Watch writes sleep to the Health app, the app reads those sleep records. Nothing else is read from Health, and nothing is written to it.
  • Morning check-ins. Your rating of the night, your estimate of how long you slept, and the night the app then showed you.
  • Tests. The results of the 1-minute tests you take, in the morning check-in or any time: when you took each one, and each answer with its time.
  • Charging and battery. When the phone is plugged in or unplugged, and the battery level at each upload.
  • Screen lock state. When the phone is locked or unlocked while the app is running.
  • Device and app details. The iPhone model, iOS version, app version, time zone and language setting, and the notification token that lets our server wake the app.
  • Your Prolific ID. Entered once at set-up. The data are filed under it.

The app does not collect your location, which apps you use, or what is on your screen.

Background activity

Between opens, iOS may wake the app briefly so it can keep recording movement and send data. These wakes show nothing on screen.

How the data are used and shared

For our study of how sleep relates to phone use at night, as its consent form describes. The data are not sold and not used for advertising or marketing. Information without identifiers may be used in future studies.

By consenting, you allow us to share the data with other institutions for research. Every institution that receives the data must protect them at least as this policy does.

Where and how long the data are kept

On the servers of our hosting provider, sent over an encrypted connection. Data sent while the app cannot reach the server are held on your phone and sent later. We keep the data for as long as our analyses need them.

Your choices

  • Permissions. Health and Motion & Fitness access can be withdrawn at any time in the iPhone's Settings; the app then collects nothing from those sources.
  • Withdrawing. Delete the app to stop all collection. Data sent before then may still be included in the analysis, as the consent form states.
  • Questions and deletion requests. Message us through Prolific or write to nicolasgoulartdemoura@gmail.com.

We may change this policy. Last updated 5 October 2026.
